
==============================
 My Open Source Manifesto
 Generated on: 26 March 2026
 Author: hpwindows
==============================

I believe in the power of open source.
Every day I rely on Git — a tool built freely and shared openly by developers around the world.
To me, freedom means Choice — and that is exactly what open source represents.
It is the freedom to use, study, modify and share software without restriction.

One day I plan to build A student collaboration tool and release it to the world for free.
Because knowledge grows when it is shared, not when it is locked away.
Standing on the shoulders of giants like Linus Torvalds and Richard Stallman,
I commit to giving back to the community that gave so much to me.

Open source is not just a license. It is a philosophy. It is a movement. It is the future.
